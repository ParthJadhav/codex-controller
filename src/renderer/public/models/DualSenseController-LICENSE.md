## Modifications

The downloadable GLB was retrieved through the Objaverse mirror of the
creator's downloadable Sketchfab model and is rendered directly by Three.js.
At runtime, the app decomposes the source mesh by connected topology and
promotes the original button, D-pad, stick, touchpad, utility-button, shoulder,
and trigger islands into semantic interactive mesh nodes. Material calibration,
procedural face-button symbols, status-light geometry, and an additive outline
are applied at runtime. The original geometry and embedded texture maps remain
bundled in the GLB.

The attribution, source link, and license link are shown in the app's
Preferences screen.
